Overview

The Ultimate Valley Experience

The Adventure Seeker package is our most comprehensive exploration of Emerald Valley, designed for travelers who want to experience everything this remarkable destination has to offer. Over five unforgettable days, you'll traverse alpine meadows, navigate ancient forests, and stand atop peaks that offer panoramic views stretching for hundreds of kilometers.

This carefully curated journey combines physical adventure with moments of serene beauty. You'll challenge yourself with guided summit expeditions, get up close with the valley's diverse wildlife, and end each day in comfort at our luxury tent camp, where gourmet meals and warm hospitality await.

Whether you're an experienced adventurer or trying outdoor activities for the first time, our expert guides ensure every experience is safe, accessible, and truly memorable. Small group sizes mean personalized attention and the flexibility to adapt to your interests and abilities.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

This package is suitable for anyone with moderate fitness. The summit day involves approximately 6-7 hours of hiking with significant elevation gain, but no technical climbing skills are required. We recommend being able to walk 10km comfortably. Our guides can adjust the pace based on group ability, and there are always alternative activities available if anyone prefers to skip certain activities.
We provide a detailed packing list after booking. Essentials include: broken-in hiking boots, layered clothing, waterproof jacket, sun protection, and a daypack (25-30L). All technical equipment (helmets, harnesses, climbing gear) is provided. Gear rental is available if needed. Temperature can vary significantly, so layers are essential.
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before departure for a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours receive 50% refund or 100% credit towards a future booking. In case of extreme weather conditions requiring cancellation by us, you'll receive a full refund or free rescheduling. We strongly recommend travel insurance for additional protection.

Prepare

How to Prepare

Physical Prep

Start cardio training 4-6 weeks before. Aim for 3-4 sessions per week including stairs and incline walking.

Gear Check

Test all gear before arrival. Break in hiking boots with several long walks to prevent blisters.

Health

Consult your doctor if you have any conditions. Bring personal medications and inform us of allergies.
