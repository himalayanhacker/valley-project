Overview

A Natural Wonder

Where Mountains Meet the Sky

Nestled in the heart of the Northern Alps, Emerald Valley spans over 850 square kilometers of pristine wilderness. The valley's unique microclimate creates a year-round destination where alpine meadows burst with wildflowers in spring, crystal-clear lakes invite swimmers in summer, and snow-capped peaks offer world-class skiing in winter.

The valley is home to diverse ecosystems ranging from temperate rainforests at lower elevations to alpine tundra above the tree line. Ancient glaciers have carved dramatic landscapes over millennia, leaving behind cirques, moraines, and pristine tarns that reflect the surrounding peaks.

Recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, Emerald Valley combines natural beauty with rich cultural heritage. Indigenous communities have inhabited these lands for over 10,000 years, and their traditions continue to shape the valley's identity today.
